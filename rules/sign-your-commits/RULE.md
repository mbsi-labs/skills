# Sign your commits

Every commit you make names you. End the commit message with a trailer line that gives your agent name and model, just above the `Co-Authored-By` line:

    Agent: <name> (Claude Opus 5.5)

Use the name your home gives you (its folder name). A subagent or an unnamed session writes its lead's name and "subagent". A commit you make for a neighbour still names you.

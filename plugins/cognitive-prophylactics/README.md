# Write a story your reader can use

By Mackenzie Bowes, with MBSI's agents.

Before you draft, it asks for one line: the situation your reader might meet and the move that lifts or lowers their standing in it.

What it can see: only the chat you add it to.

For your agent (the text it matches on): Write stories as cognitive prophylactics, rehearsals that let a reader live through a situation before they meet it and come away knowing what lifts status and what lowers it. Use when writing or judging any narrative meant to stick: devlogs and posts, case studies, game characters and their arcs, dialogue, scenes, or a story told to sell a product, and when deciding what a story is *for*.

### ChatGPT or Grok

Open https://mackenziebowes.com/skills/text/cognitive-prophylactics.txt, copy all of it, and paste it into a chat before your question.

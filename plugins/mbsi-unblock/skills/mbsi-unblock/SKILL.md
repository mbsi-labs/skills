# Don't block on the human

The user supervises asynchronously. Keep moving: decide, do, then show what you did and why. A wrong reversible call costs less than a stall.

- **Proceed on reversible work:** code, notes, tickets, branches, local config, and publishing or deploying a page or post (it can be taken down or redeployed). Do it, then present it with the reason in a line.
- **Settle mechanics yourself:** names, conventions, facts in the code. List the assumptions you made so each can be rejected.
- **Judge visual taste yourself:** critique it with the design skills, ship, and show the result.
- When you notice a problem, log it on the ticket and fix it in the next round.

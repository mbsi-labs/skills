The VS-Standard prompt with probability tuning is at lines 3993-4008: k responses as text + probability "relative to the full distribution", "Randomly sample the responses from the full distribution", or "with the probability of each response must be below {probability_tuning}". k = 5 is the paper's setting for creativity tasks (Table 1). The abstract reports 1.6-2.1x diversity in creative writing without loss of factual accuracy or safety.

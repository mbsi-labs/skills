# Why this piece

Readers read for a gain they can take and for standing among their peers. Answer these so the piece gives both, fast.

Before you write, answer these in writing, from the material in front of you:

1. What is the point of this piece?
2. Why would a reader care about it?
3. What is the business goal of this piece?
4. What does the fact that we made the thing it's about say about us, and why do we want to promote that?

Rules for the answers:

- Each answer cites its source: a sentence in the material, a number, a run, or the user's words. An answer with no source is a guess; read more before writing.
- The neighbour test: put the nearest similar piece in place of this one. Any answer that still fits it belongs to the category, not to this piece. Strike it and answer again.
- Name the kind: a craft showpiece (shows a novel solution generously, workflow included), a customer post (the reader's money, clients and business, not our tools), or research (the answer first, every figure attributed, a check the reader can run). The kind decides which writing-wins apply.

Read `references/model-answer.md` once: the user's own answers for one piece, the shape to match. Then write toward your answers, and chain the sections with "but" or "therefore", never "and then".

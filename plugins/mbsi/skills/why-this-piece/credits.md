Why they work (Rowan's reading):
- They are questions, not rules. A rule gets applied as a shape; a question has to be answered from the material in front of you, so it can't be pattern-matched.
- They face outward, in order: the work's point, the reader's gain, our gain, our standing. Checks and rules face the draft.
- "Why would a reader care" lands on the reader's reward, and "what does it say about us" lands on relative status (goal.md axiom).
- They come before drafting, when the answer can still change what gets written.
